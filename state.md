- Vow Exception is the only target product/runtime.
- Muramasa and Shank are research donors. Infinite Brutality is a bounded, deprecated donor specimen only.
- 3D world and bodies; 2D timing. Contact sheets remain source, approval surface, and correction language.

- Rear hand/forearm, independent shovel, foreground fingers, and supporting-hand ownership remain separately registered.
- Every move begins pose-only. No effects may compensate for a wrong pose.
- Whiff, flesh, armor, block, and earth are separate outcomes.
- Reduced-motion and reduced-flash are authored with each move.
- Accepted pixels are immutable inputs. Rejected passes remain quarantined.
- Terrain authority and append-only scar events outrank presentation.
- Hot swaps require declared grip, socket, pivot, occlusion, reach, and contact contracts.
- The world is not literally fleshpunk. Rendering may borrow material pressure; causality remains faithful to the novels.
